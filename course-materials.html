Teaching

Courses I teach, the textbook and websites I built for them, and the guides I keep for students and colleagues. Most of it is open; take what is useful.

Textbook and websites

  • Open textbook

    The Question Method of Legal Research

    2026

    Legal research as a chain of questions. Free, open, and yours to adopt for a 1L research course.

  • Interactive

    Question Method companion modules

    2026

    Hands-on labs for all seventeen chapters: rank the authorities, run the citator, catch the fabricated citation.

  • Tool

    The Workbench

    2026

    The companion site for Law Practice in the Age of AI & Big Data: modules for every role a lawyer plays with AI, as user, evaluator, builder, and steward.

  • Open textbook

    Introduction to Law Librarianship

    2021, second edition 2025

    The field’s introductory text, co-edited with Zanada Joyner. Used in law librarianship courses and for onboarding in libraries nationwide.
