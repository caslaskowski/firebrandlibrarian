Who am I? I’m a
librarian

Keep scrolling. There are six of me.

  1. librarianGet people what they need. Then fix the system so they need me less.
  2. tinkerIf the tool doesn’t exist, I build a clunky one, then a better one.
  3. leaderI start it myself. Then I find the people who make it real.
  4. creatorOpen textbooks, open data, open tools. Borrow them. Please.
  5. mentorNobody handed me the unwritten rules, so I wrote them down.
  6. advocateLatina, veteran, neurodivergent. I say so out loud so the next person can too.

Why Firebrand?

A crim law professor kept trying to get me to say I wanted the police catching criminals sooner. I never did. He called me a firebrand, and I kept it. The whole story, from 2018.
