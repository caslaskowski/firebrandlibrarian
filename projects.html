Major projects

Everything here pulls on the same three threads: legal informatics, equity, and open access. Braided together, they make a legal information system that more people can actually use. The character sheet has the complete record.

  • images/projects/future-of-law-libraries-initiative.jpg

    Six regional roundtables with partners at Suffolk, Georgia State, Stanford, Northwestern, and Ohio State, and more than 100 participants. Now in phase two: working groups building a central organization, a knowledge hub, and tiered AI training for legal information professionals.

    Initiative siteFinal report (PDF)
  • images/projects/the-workbench.jpg

    The companion site for Law Practice in the Age of AI & Big Data, the course I proposed at Duke and brought with me to Arizona. Modules for each role a lawyer plays with AI: user, evaluator, builder, steward.

    caslaskowski.github.io/ai-workbench
  • images/projects/question-method-of-legal-research.jpg

    An open textbook and interactive companion: research as a sequence of questions, not a list of databases.

    Open textbookInteractive modules
  • images/projects/introduction-to-law-librarianship.jpg

    The field’s introductory text, co-edited with Zanada Joyner. Second edition 2025. Winner of the Joseph A. Andrews Legal Literature Award and the ALL-SIS Book Award.

    Read it free
  • images/projects/law-library-fellows-program.jpg

    A funded route into the profession: mentorship, an assistantship, and explicit training on the hidden mores nobody writes down. Three open handbooks published so far, and the first two law librarians without J.D.s placed.

    The programStart your own
  • images/projects/ua-land-grant-history.jpg

    I converted BLM parcel records into PLSS designations, mapped them in ArcGIS, and calculated the land the university received: over 750,000 acres. Two AALL awards in 2024.

    ArcGIS story mapProject page
  • images/projects/supreme-court-indian-law-database.jpg

    Professor Keith Richotte and his research assistants built the dataset behind this: every Supreme Court Indian law case and how each justice voted. I advised on how to structure it and, using AI-assisted web development, built the site that makes it public. Launching 2026.

    scildb.com
  • images/projects/medical-debt-policy-scorecard.jpg

    PEW-funded empirical research ranking all 50 states’ medical debt consumer protections. National coverage, statutory change in several states, and the 2023 AALL Innovation in Technology Award.

    medicaldebtpolicyscorecard.org
  • images/projects/light-pollution-law-database.jpg

    With the International Dark Sky Association: a database of light pollution laws nationwide for advocates and scholars, plus a web map. I restructured the workflow around user research and a minimum viable product.

    DatabaseWeb map
